Droupadi Murmu is the 15th and current President of India, inaugurated on July 25, 2022. She is the first person belonging to a tribal community, 
the second woman (after Pratibha Patil), and the youngest person (at 64) to hold the office. As Head of State, she acts as the nominal executive, 
first citizen, and supreme commander of India's armed forces. The President of India is the head of state and the first citizen of the Republic of India, 
serving as the symbolic leader of the nation and the commander-in-chief of the Indian Armed Forces. The office is defined by the Constitution, 
and while many executive powers are exercised on the advice of the Prime Minister and the Council of Ministers, 
the President plays an important constitutional role in the functioning of the democratic system, including appointing key officials, 
signing bills into law, and representing India in ceremonial and diplomatic functions.
